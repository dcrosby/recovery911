**Setting up a Safe Environment for Prevention and Recovery!**

Recovery involves some amount of preparation ***before*** the crash...
Better to take a few minutes now to do a little prep than be forced to do a complete Windows reinstall later...

## Creating a Safe Environment

- Install Windows Sandbox (requires Windows Pro)
- Harden your system - a few simple procedures
- Backup your important data (family pictures, etc...)
- Debloat your system - Lose the fat
- Optimize your performance

### Prepare Your Critical Info
- Create your Recovery Toolbox
- Save your passwords
