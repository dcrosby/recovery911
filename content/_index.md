### 🧭 The Recovery Framework

**1. Avoid Trouble If Possible**  
_Prevention is the best cure—stay ahead of problems before they start._

**2. Prepare Before Trouble Hits**
- 🧾 Collect all system information  
- 📞 Keep support contacts and warranty info handy  
- 💾 Maintain reliable backups  
- ⚙️ Configure for stability  
- 🔐 Harden your system within reason  
- 📊 Monitor for early warning signs  
- 🧪 Test your recovery readiness  
- 🧰 Build your own customized recovery kit (including boot and repair media)

**3. Future Problem Categories**

These are common areas where recovery tools and strategies will be focused:

- 🧱 **Hardware Issues** — physical failures, wear and tear, or faulty components  
- 🐢 **Performance** — slow systems, lag, or resource bottlenecks  
- 🦠 **Malware** — viruses, ransomware, spyware, or other malicious threats  
- 💥 **Boot Failure** — system won’t start, stuck in loops, or missing boot files  
- 💾 **Disk or Data Corruption** — unreadable drives, file system errors, or bad sectors  
- 🕳️ **Lost Files or Data** — accidental deletion, overwritten files, or missing folders  
- 🧩 **Application Issues** — crashes, misbehavior, or failed installs  
- 🔌 **Compatibility** — software or hardware conflicts, driver mismatches  
- ⬆️ **Upgrade Issues** — failed updates, broken features, or rollback needs  
- 🧾 **Licensing** — expired keys, activation errors, or missing credentials  
- 🌐 **Networking and Communications** — connection drops, misconfigured routers, or DNS failures
